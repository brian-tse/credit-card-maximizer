How this estimate works

The options are mutually exclusive for a billing cycle. Card base rewards come from the current CardMax catalog; category bonuses, fees, annual benefits and promotional offers are excluded.

Housing-only rates are 0.5×, 0.75×, 1× and 1.25× at spending ratios of 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%. Below 25%, an eligible housing payment earns 250 points per account per cycle. No housing payment means no housing points.

Flexible earns 4% Bilt Cash on eligible everyday purchases. Housing redemptions cost $0.03 per point, up to 1 point per housing dollar. This estimate assumes the modeled Bilt Cash is available when redeemed; posting times may affect your actual result. You can choose to redeem less. Under the current Bilt Cash terms, cash expires at year end, with up to $100 carrying into the next year.

The account must be eligible and in good standing, with the required linked bank account. Housing payments must go through Bilt. Points are rounded down for this estimate. Options can generally change for the following billing cycle.
